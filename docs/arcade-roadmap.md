# Ten arcade classics to build from

Research date: 24 October 2026. Order below is our implementation priority, based on lasting recognition, strong replay loops, keyboard suitability, and variety alongside the existing portal. It is not a measured worldwide popularity ranking.

Evidence includes the [KLOV reader-selected popularity list](https://www.arcade-museum.com/top100), museum collections, and publishers' histories. KLOV's separate Top 100 is chronological, not ranked. Proposed portal mechanics below are design recommendations, not claims about the original games.

## Shortlist

| Order | Classic | Why it earns a place | Portal direction for sustained play | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Asteroids** | Splitting rocks create immediate feedback and changing movement problems. The Strong records its commercial success and freedom of play; it also appears in KLOV's reader top ten. [The Strong](https://www.museumofplay.org/games/asteroids/), [KLOV](https://www.arcade-museum.com/top100). | **Asteroid Drift**: responsive flight, rock splitting, evasive dash, distinct wave patterns, upgrade choices, and local records. Later waves should change tactics rather than only increase health. | Implemented |
| 2 | **Pac-Man** | Clear routes, pursuit, and temporary reversals of danger make a readable chase. A Hall of Fame inductee and KLOV reader favorite. [The Strong](https://www.museumofplay.org/games/pac-man/), [KLOV](https://www.arcade-museum.com/top100). | Original maze-chase game with several connected districts, enemy personalities, risky bonus routes, and locally saved district progress. | Implemented |
| 3 | **Tetris** | Simple falling-piece rules support extensive planning and recovery. The Strong identifies its persistent international popularity. [The Strong](https://www.museumofplay.org/games/tetris/). | Original falling-block game with predictable controls, placement preview, piece storage, satisfying line clears, and endurance gravity, combo goals, and earned board rescues. | Implemented |
| 4 | **Frogger** | Short crossing decisions turn moving traffic into a timing puzzle. Included in both KLOV's classics selection and the Museum of the Moving Image's arcade collection exhibition. [KLOV](https://www.arcade-museum.com/top100), [Moving Image](https://movingimage.org/archived-events/arcade-classics-video-games-from-the-collection/). | Crossing expedition through traffic, rivers, and changing hazards. Link crossings into regions with route choices and device-local checkpoints. | Implemented |
| 5 | **Bomberman** | Placement, escape routes, and acquired items create tactical local battles. Konami describes the series' broad audience and longstanding competitive-party appeal. [Konami](https://www.konami.com/crossmedia/us/en/products/bomberman/). | Solo arena ladder against escape-planning bots, plus two players on one keyboard. Mix destructible layouts, timed explosives, pickups, and match series. | Implemented |
| 6 | **Bubble Bobble** | Capturing then bursting enemies combines action with positioning. Taito documents its arcade origin, sequels, and stage-based structure. [Taito](https://www.taito.co.jp/en/BB4F/console). | Original bubble-platform game with solo/cooperative rooms, multi-enemy chains, air-current choices, and a campaign of authored rooms. | Implemented |
| 7 | **Donkey Kong** | Climbing, jumping, and dodging visible hazards make progress easy to understand. It appears in KLOV's reader top ten and the Moving Image arcade exhibition. [KLOV](https://www.arcade-museum.com/top100), [Moving Image](https://movingimage.org/archived-events/arcade-classics-video-games-from-the-collection/). | Original climbing platformer with several construction sites, moving platforms, different hazard behaviors, and saved chapter progress. | Implemented |
| 8 | **Galaga** | Formation shooting gains variety from enemies leaving their ranks to attack. Its recognition is supported by KLOV's reader-selected list. [KLOV](https://www.arcade-museum.com/top100). | A dedicated formation-shooter campaign would need capture/rescue decisions, authored dive patterns, and challenge rounds to distinguish it from existing Star Patrol. | Implemented |
| 9 | **OutRun** | Branching routes give driving runs different destinations. Sega's own history describes its branching courses and enduring score competition. [Sega](https://www.sega.jp/history/arcade/product/9321/). | Original scenic arcade drive with clear steering, traffic, route forks, changing road conditions, and a linked touring run. | Implemented |
| 10 | **Metal Slug** | Readable running, shooting, weapon changes, and vehicles support varied action. SNK documents the original arcade title and the series' continuing audience at its 30th anniversary. [SNK game page](https://www.snk-corp.co.jp/us/games/acaneogeo/metalslug/), [SNK anniversary](https://www.snk-corp.co.jp/anniversary/metalslug30th/). | Original run-and-gun campaign with responsive aim, enemy telegraphs, weapon tradeoffs, vehicles, and saved missions. | Implemented |

## Delivery scope

The owner subsequently authorized implementation of **all ten games**. Build and verify the complete shortlist, preserving distinct mechanics, long-play progression, losses, retries, and local checkpoints. The earlier single-game assessment gate no longer applies.

Use original portal names, code, artwork, stages, and audio. The classic titles identify the source of gameplay inspiration. Keep existing games available and follow [the game design standard](game-design.md), including client-only play, standalone modules, integrated settings, sharp canvases, local scores, and worthwhile 15–30 minute sessions.
